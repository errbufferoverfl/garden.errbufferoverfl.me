Welcome to *Creative Dispatch*, a lightweight glimpse into my studio life! This free update is a quick snapshot of what I’ve been working on, giving you a taste of the projects and ideas I’m exploring. I’m excited to share these snippets with you, so let’s dive right in!

## Art Class Adventures

Since the start of the year, I’ve been taking art classes at a local studio, working primarily in colored pencils and, more recently, oil pastels. One of my latest projects has been an oil pastel illustration of Turby, my 4-foot Furby muse. Rendering the fluffy fur was challenging, but it’s been a great way to improve my oil pastel techniques and learn how to work with this medium.

![A vibrant, surreal oil pastel drawing of a creature resembling a Furby with bright pink and blue fur. The background is black, and white blocks with letters on them spell "NO GODS" on the left and "NO MASTERS" on the right.](october-2024--creative-dispatch.jpeg)

We’re now moving on to oil painting, and I’m super excited to dive into that next!

## Lino Print Projects

Recently, I also took a private lino printing class to work on a reduction print technique. It was a fun session, although we struggled a bit with the registration, leading to some quirky rabbit prints.

![A series of hand-printed artworks depicting a brown rabbit in profile, set against a pastel green and pink checkered background. The prints are laid out in a row on an orange mat, showing slight variations in ink application.](october-2024--creative-dispatch-1.jpeg)

The process helped me debug some ink application issues I’d been having, and after realizing the problem was with the surface I was using (a flexible glass panel), I switched to a high-gloss tile, and everything started working smoothly.

## Zines

Outside of class, I’ve been working on a new zine about my favorite comfort food—hot chips! Originally, I planned it as a mini zine, but after test printing, I decided to expand it into a full 8-page zine. I’ve added some extra content to really flesh it out, and I’m excited to share it as a free digital download for subscribers or available to buy from my Ko-Fi store!

## Flood the Post

I also recently participated in the Flood the Post initiative, which aims to send a powerful message to politicians about ending the genocide in Gaza. Physical mail has a way of making an impact that emails can’t, so I spent a week designing postcards featuring a pomegranate with the text "FREE PALESTINE" and "CEASEFIRE NOW."

![A collection of protest postcards featuring a red illustration of a pomegranate and the words "FREE PALESTINE" and "CEASEFIRE NOW" in bold, blocky letters. The postcards are displayed on a pastel-colored desk, with some text in red and others in green, referencing the colors of the Palestinian flag.](october-2024--creative-dispatch-2.jpeg)

My partner and I printed and sent out about 14 postcards to various politicians, and it felt amazing to be part of something so communal and impactful.

## Creative Side Quests

Between art projects, I’ve also been busy writing government submissions. My most recent ones are for the Privacy Act Review and the Cyber Security Legislative Package. While they are made public after a decision by the Committee, you can find them posted on my personal website:

- [Submission to the Privacy Act Review](/engineering/2024-10-submission-in-response-to-the-privacy-amendment-bill)
- [Submission to the Cyber Security Legislative Package](/engineering/2024-10-submission-in-response-to-the-cyber-security-legislative-package-2024)

While they’ve been mentally exhausting, the process of advocating for change has fueled my creative energy, helping me turn back to projects like a new zine *Changing the Ratio: It’s More than Just Conferences, Folks!*, which explores gender disparities in tech spaces.

Thank you so much for following along on this creative journey! Your interest and support mean a lot to me, and it keeps me motivated to continue growing and sharing my work. That’s all for this edition of Creative Dispatch—until next time, stay radical!
